Overview

"Network Default" is the standard Caller ID setting provided by your mobile network. Choosing it means your outgoing calls will show your number according to the network's default rule (usually your number is displayed), rather than a custom "Show" or "Hide" setting you may have selected before.

You can reset your Caller ID to Network Default in three ways: calling customer care and following the automated phone menu (IVR), dialing a quick USSD code, or using your carrier's app/online account. All three are covered below — start with whichever is most convenient.

Step-by-Step Phone Menu Navigation

Call your carrier's customer care line and follow this call flow. Menu wording varies slightly by carrier, but the structure below is the common path.

Step 1 · Dial

Call Customer Care

Dial your carrier's customer care number from the phone line you want to update (e.g. 111, 123, or the toll-free support number printed on your SIM packaging or bill).

↓
Step 2 · Press 1

Choose Your Language

"Press 1 for English, Press 2 for [local language]."

↓
Step 3 · Main Menu

Select "Phone & Network Settings"

  • 1 – Account & Billing
  • 2 – Technical Support
  • 3 – Phone & Network Settings
  • 4 – New Services
  • 0 – Speak to an Agent

Press 3.

↓
Step 4 · Submenu

Select "Caller ID Settings"

  • 1 – Call Forwarding
  • 2 – Caller ID Settings
  • 3 – Voicemail Settings
  • 9 – Back to Main Menu

Press 2.

↓
Step 5 · Caller ID Menu

Select "Reset to Network Default"

  • 1 – Always Show My Number
  • 2 – Always Hide My Number
  • 3 – Reset to Network Default
  • 9 – Back

Press 3.

↓
Step 6 · Confirm

Confirm the Change

"You have selected to reset your Caller ID to Network Default. Press 1 to confirm, or 2 to cancel." Press 1 to confirm.

↓
Done

Confirmation

The system reads back: "Your Caller ID has been reset to Network Default." You will also receive a confirmation SMS. The change typically applies within a few minutes.

Quick Dial Codes

If your carrier supports USSD self-service, you can skip the phone menu entirely.

USSD Self-Service Menu

Dial:

*123#

Then follow the on-screen prompts: Settings → Caller ID → Network Default.

Direct Caller ID Reset

Some networks support a direct reset code, dialed like a phone call:

#31#

This clears any custom Caller ID override and restores the network's default behavior.

Exact codes differ by carrier and country. If a code doesn't work, use the phone menu in the previous section or ask an agent to confirm your network's code.

Mobile App & Online Account

Carrier App

Open your carrier's app and navigate to:

Settings → Call Settings → Additional Settings → Caller ID → Network Default

Tap Save to apply.

Online Self-Service Portal

Log in to your account online, open Line Settings for the number you want to update, and select Network Default under Caller ID.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Network Default" actually do?

It removes any custom Show/Hide override on your line and lets the network apply its standard Caller ID behavior, which in most cases means your number is displayed to the person you call.

How long does the change take to apply?

Usually a few minutes. If it hasn't applied after 30 minutes, restart your phone or contact customer care.

Can I switch back later?

Yes. Follow the same phone menu or app path and choose "Always Hide My Number" or "Always Show My Number" instead of Network Default.
